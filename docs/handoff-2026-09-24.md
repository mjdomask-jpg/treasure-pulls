## What the form must do

This is gathered from the decisions above so the form work can start from one
list. The detail is in the backlog's entry-form item.

- **Required:** an event, the reporter's name, and at least one item. Nothing
  else. The submit button works as soon as those three are filled in.
- **Sections by treatment:** Standard, Condensed packs and Pack substitutes,
  chosen with tick-boxes that start ticked. An item's treatment is the section
  it was entered in. In-person and mail events show only Standard, with no
  tick-boxes.
- **Count boxes that differ by section**, chosen by how often each item
  appeared in 2026 submissions under that treatment. Players type how many are
  in each stack. *(Corrected later on 2026-09-24. An earlier version of this
  list gave every section the same boxes, including 10x Treasure Chips and the
  named Ultra Rare-or-better items. Measured, those are rare enough to belong
  in search.)*
  - **Standard (6):** Rare, Uncommon, Monster Trophy (Monster Bit), both chase
    sets, and the treasure-chest-only Rare (`Mystery Treasure Chest Rare` until
    2027's is revealed).
  - **Condensed packs (16):** Monster Trophy, both chase sets, the
    treasure-chest-only Rare, and Trade 1 and Trade 2 including the 1,000 GP
    bar, as a compact grid.
  - **Pack substitutes (8):** Trade 1. This is the owner's hypothesis, with no
    2026 data behind it.
  - The lists are `data/seed/form_box.csv`. The measurements are in
    data-model § 4, *Which count boxes each section shows*.
- **An "add another item" search** for everything else, such as Paragon or the
  100,000 GP bar. Opened empty, it suggests the section's next most common
  items. It matches on aliases, so `DS` finds Dwarven Steel. It leaves
  out anything that already has a count box: standard-set tokens and group
  members.
- **One hint the player sees:** condensed treasure that doesn't come to a
  multiple of 10. They can dismiss it.
- **Two lines of static help text:** items from redeeming a 10x chip go under
  Standard; don't include your free Participation token.
- **Silent snap:** a name that differs from an existing one only in capitals or
  spacing is replaced with the existing spelling.
- **Never** use the words "total pulls". Design for a phone first.

## The things that will silently go wrong

- **The 2027 catalog is missing its treasure-only tokens until after January
  2027:** chase sets, Monster Trophies and treasure-only Rares. Run
  `node scripts/fetch_catalog.mjs 2027`, then rename the Mystery placeholders in
  place and keep the old names as aliases. The Monster Trophy group fills itself
  in from the re-fetch.
- **Before fetching any new year (2028 onward), add that year's three rows to
  `bonus_tier.csv`.** Without them, the 2k Bonus token is labelled an ordinary
  Ultra Rare, because tokendb calls it one.
- **A new tokendb rarity label stops the fetch** on purpose, and names the
  tokens. Map the label in `fetch_catalog.mjs` (`RARITY` or `CANONICAL`) per
  data-model § 4. Don't just let it through: that's how two Arcanum labels went
  unmapped until 09-23.

## Traps, all of which cost time this session

- **"Merged" is not proof.** Twice this session, PRs described as merged were
  still open, and the next branch had to be stacked on top of them. Before
  branching, check `gh pr view <n> --json state` and `git log origin/main`.
- **Never restore a file with `git checkout -- <file>` after testing a
  change.** It also throws away every uncommitted edit to that file, and it did
  exactly that to the fetcher once. To test a check with a planted bad row,
  copy the file to the scratchpad first and copy it back afterwards, then
  compare the two with `cmp`.
- **Re-run the fetcher to prove the catalogs.** Output must be byte-identical
  to what's committed. This proves the committed script is the one that
  generated the committed data.
- **The auction project** (`C:\claude\site`) was read at origin/main `917b0e7`
  for the bonus-tier display names and the alias spellings. Read origin/main
  there, never the local branch.
- **The truedungeon.com events page has copy-paste errors.** Two names are
  spelled two ways, and Dark Archive's ticket line mentions Dead Evil. The
  headings are the names to use.
- **CSVs are pinned to LF**; Markdown and `.mjs` are not, so git's CRLF warnings
  on those are expected. Workbooks are gitignored, and their paths are in this
  project's memory directory, not the repo, because the repo is public.
