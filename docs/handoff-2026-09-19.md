## The finding that changes the model

**Measured 2026-09-19 from `2026 Treasure Pull Records.xlsx`.** This is the one
piece of new evidence produced this session and it is the reason the schema needs
more than a tidy-up.

The review's condensed-pack fact turns the sheet's `Total Pulls` column into two
different units depending on the row:

| Treatment | rows whose `Total Pulls` is a multiple of 10 |
|---|---|
| Condensed | **98 of 107 (92%)** |
| Non-condensed | 24 of 65 (37%) — clustering on 20, 24, 25, 26, 27, 52, 54 |

Condensed rows count **packs × 10**. Non-condensed rows count **individual
pulls**. One run samples the pool 27 times and yields either 27 items or 10 items
depending on a checkbox.

The resulting bias, across the four 2026 events carrying both treatments
(8,375 items: 4,981 condensed, 3,394 non-condensed):

| Token | rate, non-cond. | rate, cond. | ratio | cond. × 10/27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Golem Chaser | 4.74% | 13.17% | 2.78 | **4.88%** |
| Herald's Chaser | 4.60% | 11.80% | 2.57 | **4.37%** |
| Monster Bit | 8.54% | 26.30% | 3.08 | **9.74%** |

Three independent tokens each imply ≈2.7 draws per condensed item. That is 27/10
recovered from the data, and applying it reconciles the two treatments.

**Consequences:**

- The workbook's per-event drop-rate percentages are wrong by ~2.7× on every
  condensed row, and condensed is already **59% of recorded 2026 items**.
- `data-model.md` stores `quantity` and computes totals, which is right, but a
  rate needs a denominator and `SUM(quantity)` is the denominator for
  non-condensed rows only.
- `domain-context.md`'s line that condensed and non-condensed "are two samples of
  the same treasure" is true of the **pool** and false of the **observation
  process**. That sentence needs qualifying.

Three quantities need distinct names in the schema and the prose, because the
workbook has one word for all three:

- **draws** — times the pool was sampled. The denominator for every rate.
- **items** — physical tokens received. `SUM(pull.quantity)`.
- **entitlement** — pulls earned from play.

`draws = entitlement + granted` (a `10x Pull` is one draw granting ten more).
`items = draws` non-condensed; `items < draws` condensed.

`10x Pull` appears **once in all of 2026**, so modelling it correctly is cheap
now and never gets cheaper.

## Open questions — awaiting the owner, who is answering tomorrow

**The owner has these and said the answers will be long. Do not re-ask them, and
do not design around a guess.** Numbering matches how they were asked.

Blocking the condensed / denominator design:

1. **Condensed packaging** — is it one 10-token pack per max-treasure run, so 27
   draws → 10 items? Inferred from the multiples-of-10 evidence and the 2.7×
   ratio, not stated. And at 30 pulls in 2027, does the pack stay 10?
2. **Breakpoint bonus treasure** — do party breakpoints (1–5, 6–9, 10) grant
   **extra draws from the same pool**, or **specific known tokens**? If specific,
   they are deterministic rewards sitting in the data as if random, and must be
   excluded from the estimate.
3. **Are the condensed conversion ratios published or community-known per year?**
   Decides whether condensed submissions contribute to Rare/Uncommon and
   trade-good estimates, or only to everything else.

Blocking the token catalog, the seed vocabularies and the entry form:

4. **Granularity for the bulk categories** — Rare and Uncommon are most of the
   volume, 40 of each per year. Count, or itemized by name? Same for chase pieces
   (variant-level would serve the "chase tokens in the wild" goal, at 40 variants
   to pick from).
5. **Previous-year Relics and Legendaries** — require the specific token and its
   year, or accept "a Relic, 2024"?
7. **Will players reliably report runs or total pulls?** The whole denominator
   argument rests on this being one easy field. If a guildmaster batching six
   people's Gen Con treasure cannot answer it, the fallback needs designing.

Not blocking:

6. **tokendb slugs as `token.external_slug`** — stable, and acceptable to its
   owner? Cheapest path to the shared-vocabulary goal.
8. **Minor** — all six 2026 sheets carry the Condensed column, but Gen Con and
   Scarlet Moon have zero condensed rows. Is condensed offered at in-person
   events at all, or merely unused there?
